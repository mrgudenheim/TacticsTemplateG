SCOPE — WHAT THIS FILE DOES AND DOES NOT COVER
================================================================================

This MIT grant covers `godot-learning/` ONLY. It does not change the repository
root `LICENSE` (GPL-3.0-or-later), which continues to govern every other
directory in this monorepo, and it is narrower than that root LICENSE rather
than an exception to it: the root remains GPL because the codebase links JUCE
(GPL v3+) in `exmateria-daw-plugin/` and ports EDC/ECC code from FFTPatcher
(GPL v3+) in `fft-iso-patcher/`. Neither of those reaches this directory.

TWO SUBDIRECTORIES INSIDE `godot-learning/` ARE EXCLUDED FROM THIS GRANT. They
are third-party or third-party-derived, are not the copyright holder's to
relicense, and keep the licences they already carry:

  godot-learning/vendor/
      Mirrors of packages whose originals are GPL-licensed or GPL-derived:

        vendor/exmateria_sound/     the PSX sound runtime
        vendor/exmateria_spu/       the SPU extension, whose native sources port
                                    PCSX-Redux's GPL-2.0 SPU — the spu.cc noise
                                    generator, the adsr.cc envelope, reverb.cc,
                                    and kode54's gauss interpolation table. Its
                                    own NOTICE records the provenance. (The one
                                    PCSX-Redux file that IS MIT, supportpsx/
                                    adpcm.{h,cc}, is vendored separately under
                                    exmateria-sound/src/vendor/ and carries its
                                    own MIT header.)
        vendor/fft_iso_patcher/     ports of FFTPatcher (GPL v3+)

  godot-learning/tools/data/vendor/
      FFTPatcher's upstream `EventCommands.xml` and
      `BattleConditionalCommands.xml` data tables (GPL v3+).

Everything else under `godot-learning/` — including all ten `addons/exmateria_*`
packages, `src/`, `tests/`, `tools/` outside the vendor directory above, and
`docs/` — is the copyright holder's own work and is offered under the MIT terms
at the top of this file.

Verified when this file was written: no file under `godot-learning/` outside
`vendor/` carries a third-party copyright, SPDX identifier or GPL header.
